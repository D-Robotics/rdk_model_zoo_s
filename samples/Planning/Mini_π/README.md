# 1. Introduction

The sample of this repository is the whole process of efficiently deploying the Gaoqing Electromechanical XiaoPi robot based on RDK S100. 

In the past decade, bipedal and humanoid robots have witnessed explosive growth in technology and applications. From Boston Dynamics' Atlas to Tesla's Optimus, and then to the catch - up of a series of domestic start - up teams, humanoid robots are moving from the laboratory to the eve of industrialization. They are not only a symbol of the integration of AI and robotics technology, but also regarded as "new species" in the fields of future services, manufacturing, and healthcare, carrying the millennium dream of human beings for intelligent mechanical partners. 

Gaoqing Electromechanical is a High Tech company focusing on the R & D of core technologies of intelligent robots, committed to creating robot products with independent core components and highly integrated solutions. The company's team has profound mechatronics and embedded system development capabilities, and its products are widely used in education and scientific research, industrial automation and service robots. Gaoqing Electromechanical adheres to the concept of "technology based, innovation driven" to promote the high-quality development of the domestic robot industry chain. 

"Little Pi" is a lightweight biped robot platform launched by Gaoqing Electromechanical, integrating motion control, sensing and intelligent interaction. The robot has the ability to walk with humanoid gait and adopts modular design, which is convenient for colleges and universities and scientific research institutions to carry out secondary development and algorithm verification. With the open software interface and powerful hardware platform support, "Little Pi" has become an ideal carrier for the cross research of artificial intelligence, control theory and robotics. 

The RDK S100 is a high-performance embedded computing platform launched by Digua Robotics. It has an AI inference capability of up to 80 TOPS (trillion operations per second) and is suitable for high-load scenarios such as Edge Computing, robot vision, and SLAM navigation. The platform is compact in size and optimized for power consumption. It is equipped with multiple high-speed interfaces and is compatible with a variety of mainstream sensors and controllers. It is an indispensable core module for building intelligent machine systems. 

This project aims to achieve the deep integration of the RDK S100 high computing power platform and the high-powered electromechanical biped robot "Little Pi" to create a high-performance biped robot intelligent control system for the future. By using the RDK S100 as the core computing power support platform for "Little Pi", it not only provides powerful AI inference and real-time computing capabilities, but also enables it to have "brain-like" intelligence such as autonomous environmental perception, behavior planning and dynamic control.
